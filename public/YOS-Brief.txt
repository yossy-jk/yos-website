# Your Office Space

Your Office Space is an outsourced property team. We partner with businesses to take the strain out of growing.

Find It, Fit It Out, Furnish It and help it Flourish.

Established in 2025. Newcastle NSW. Joe Kelley brings a decade of commercial property and workplace experience; that is Joe’s experience, not the age of YOS.

## Four pillars
- Find It: tenant representation in NSW and commercial buyers agency, particularly for businesses moving from leasing to buying. https://www.yourofficespace.au/tenant-rep and https://www.yourofficespace.au/buyers-agency
- Fit It Out: client-side fitout project management. YOS is not the builder or head contractor. Appointed appropriately licensed contractors perform construction. https://www.yourofficespace.au/office-fitout
- Furnish It: furniture projects and the online store https://www.yourofficefurniture.com.au. https://www.yourofficespace.au/furniture
- Flourish It: commercial cleaning by the Flourish Team in Newcastle CBD and Lake Macquarie, with monthly quality assurance. After-move-in services include plant hire, test and tag, fire and first aid compliance coordination, confirmed for the site and provider scope. https://www.yourofficespace.au/flourish
