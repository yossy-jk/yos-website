# Your Office Space

> Newcastle-based commercial property and workplace advisory serving Newcastle, the Hunter Region and Sydney. One accountable partner from the first conversation to final delivery. Four core services — tenant representation, office fit out project management, commercial office furniture, and commercial cleaning — plus commercial property purchase (buyers agency) and HubSpot/RevOps consulting. We work exclusively for the tenant or buyer. Never for the landlord or vendor.

An office move, fit out or commercial purchase has a fixed budget — think of it as a pie. From the moment the project starts, up to 30 contractors, suppliers and agents all need to eat from it. The greedy ones take more than their share early, and the business owner doesn't see it until quality drops at the back end and the budget is gone. Worse, leadership gets pulled away from revenue work to manage a process they were never equipped for. Your Office Space gets in early, before the pie starts shrinking — so the owner stays focused on what they do best and the budget is protected.

We are not a real estate agency, a fit out company or a cleaning business on their own — we are all of these working together under one relationship. No one in our market offers the full end-to-end ecosystem under one trusted partner.
