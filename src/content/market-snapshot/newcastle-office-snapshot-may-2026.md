### Historical May 2026 edition | Prepared by Your Office Space

## EDITORIAL UPDATE — 19 OCTOBER 2026

This edition originally included vacancy, rental, supply and absorption figures without sufficiently traceable supporting evidence. Those numerical claims have been removed. The May edition remains available as a leasing discussion guide; it should not be used as a current market report or valuation.

## COMPARE THE SPACE YOUR BUSINESS CAN ACTUALLY USE

A city-wide vacancy figure does not establish the availability of premises that meet your requirements. Start with the usable area, permitted use, access, parking, services and timing your business needs. Record the date each shortlisted option was checked and whether it is genuinely available.

**Useful question:** Which comparable premises meet our non-negotiables today, and what evidence supports each asking term?

## PUT RENTS ON THE SAME BASIS

Record the quoted annual rent, floor area, whether the rent is gross or net, GST treatment, outgoings, reviews and incentives. A lower headline rent can still produce a higher total occupancy cost. Ask for written inclusions and exclusions rather than relying on the lease label.

Separate asking terms from agreed transaction evidence. Note the evidence date, premises condition, term, incentives and any limitations. A comparison should explain differences, not just list a price per square metre.

## CHECK SUPPLY AND TIMING

Distinguish an available building from a proposed development. Confirm approval status, construction progress and the delivery date with the relevant parties before treating future supply as a workable relocation option.

**Useful question:** What is our fallback if access, approvals or building works are delayed?

## BUILD A RENEWAL AND RELOCATION COMPARISON

- Record your expiry, option notice windows and any notice method requirements with your solicitor.
- Request current renewal terms and compare them with realistic alternatives.
- Include fitout, furniture, moving, business disruption and lease-exit obligations in the decision.
- Confirm responsibility for landlord consent, approvals and specialist advice before committing.
- Use a dated comparison matrix, with assumptions and unanswered questions visible.

## EVIDENCE TO REQUEST

Ask for dated comparable terms, the source and coverage of any vacancy report, current outgoings information and written landlord proposals. Where transaction information is confidential or incomplete, record that limitation. No saving or negotiation outcome is guaranteed.
